# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2025-12-06
App: Adaire SDKs (adaire_sdk_app)
Developer: Hello Adaire

## Information We Collect
- **Camera Access:** Used solely to scan barcodes/QR codes and credit cards. Images and frames are processed on-device.
- **Scan Results:**
  - **Barcode/QR:** Decoded type and value are displayed to the user and are not transmitted off the device.
  - **Credit Card:** Detected card number, brand, and expiry month/year are processed on-device and shown to the user. The app does not store or transmit this data.
- **Security Checks:** In production mode, the app may detect whether a debugger is attached to help protect users. No personal data is collected or transmitted during this check.
- **No Account Data:** The app does not create accounts or collect names, emails, addresses, or device identifiers.
- **No Background Collection:** The app does not collect data in the background.

## How We Use Information
- **Provide Functionality:** Perform on-device scanning and display results to the user.
- **Safety & Integrity:** Detect active debugging in production mode to help safeguard the app experience.

## Data Sharing
- **No Sale of Data:** We do not sell any data.
- **No Third-Party Sharing:** We do not share data with advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties.
- **Service Providers:** None used for data processing; scanning runs on-device.

## Data Storage & Retention
- **No Persistent Storage:** Scan outputs are not persistently stored by the app.
- **Transient Display:** Results are visible only during the session (e.g., dialog display) and are cleared when dialogs are closed or the app is closed.

## Permissions
- **Camera (`CAMERA`):** Required solely for scanning. No other sensitive permissions are requested.

## Children’s Privacy
This app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us to remove it.

## Security
- **On-Device Processing:** Scans occur locally to reduce exposure.
- **Debugger Detection:** In production mode, the app checks for attached debuggers to mitigate tampering risks.
- **Best Efforts:** We use reasonable safeguards, though no method is 100% secure.

## International Use
The app does not transfer data across borders, as it does not transmit personal data off-device.

## Your Choices
- **Camera Permission:** You can disable camera access in device settings, which will disable scanning features.
- **Uninstall:** You can uninstall the app at any time.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app or legal requirements. We will update the “Effective Date” and publish the latest version with releases.
